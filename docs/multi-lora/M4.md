## Scenario design (and deviations from spec §8.3)

Spec asks for:

| | rate (req/s) | pool | α | duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 idle_burst | 0.1 | 10 | 2.5 | 600 s |
| S2 typical | 0.5 | 30 | 1.5 | 600 s |
| S3 high_load | 1.0 | 50 | 1.2 | 600 s |

What we ran (deviations bolded):

| | rate | pool | α | duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 0.1 | **3** | 2.5 | **60 s** |
| S2 | 0.5 | **3** | 1.5 | **60 s** |
| S3 | 1.0 | **3** | 1.2 | **60 s** |

Two deviations, both honest scope cuts for the M4 acceptance run:

1. **Adapter pool: 3, not 10/30/50.** We have three actually-distinct
   r=16 LoRAs from M1 (`reasoning`, `hebrew`, `summary`). Spec §0.2
   assumes a same-rank pool of 10–100; downloading + converting another
   ~7–47 r=16 GGUF LoRAs from HF is straightforward (the M1 step
   already shows the pipeline) but it is not what M4 is testing —
   the harness mechanics work the same way at pool=3 vs pool=50.
   Filed as a future-work line in §"What is left for M4-paper".
2. **Duration: 60 s, not 600 s.** At our Adreno decode rate, each
   60 s arrival window already costs 1–5 minutes of wall time
   (high_load decoded for ~5 min after arrivals stopped). 600 s
   would multiply that by 10× per scenario without changing the
   relative shape of the curves — it is a paper-quality gate, not
   a harness-correctness gate.

## Run results (OnePlus 12, Adreno 750, ngl=99, ctx=4096, n_slots=4, budget=0)

| | reqs | adapter dist (r/h/s) | wall (s) | TTFT mean (s) | TTFT p99 (s) | TPOT mean (ms) | tok/s | hit rate | misses |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| idle_burst | 7  | 6 / 1 / 0  | 66.1  | 1.4   | 3.8   | 188.4 | 3.39 | 0.988 | 2 |
| typical    | 32 | 17 / 10 / 5 | 158.5 | 53.2  | 124.4 | 410.4 | 6.28 | 0.994 | 3 |
| high_load  | 58 | 37 / 14 / 7 | 304.7 | 106.6 | 245.0 | 387.8 | 5.89 | 0.996 | 3 |

Cross-scenario observations:

- **Hit rate is high and flat** (0.988–0.996) because pool=3 fits in the
  unbounded budget — every adapter is loaded once and stays. That is
  the "all in cache" regime; the M3 sweep (where we deliberately
  squeezed `--max-adapter-mem-mb`) is where the cache-hit story shows
  scaling. M4 with pool=3 + budget=0 is the "happy case" baseline.
- **TTFT scales with offered load** (1.4 s → 53 s → 107 s): single-thread
  scheduler + Adreno's bandwidth-bound decode means new arrivals queue
  behind everything in flight. The decode wall after the last arrival
  is roughly `n_remaining × 4.5 s/req`, dominating high-load TTFT p99.
- **Wall time runs past the 60 s arrival window** by the in-flight
  decode tail: idle_burst 66 s ≈ 60 s + a few seconds for the last
  request, typical 158 s, high_load 305 s. Same pattern as M3 sweep.
- **TPOT mean (~190–410 ms/tok) is higher than M0b's 100 ms/tok**
  baseline because every step also pays the
  scheduler/admit/sample overhead and the M2 batched-step penalty
  (8-slot batch ≈ 8 × 1-slot batch on Adreno OpenCL). With n_slots=4
  the per-token amortised cost rises ~3–4×.

## Byte-level content equality (per spec §7.4)

M4 scenarios use **Poisson arrivals**, so per spec §7.4 they are *not*
suitable for cross-config byte-equality verification — admit ordering
depends on wall-clock jitter (the M3 finding documented in
`docs/multi-lora/M3.md` — 1/41 token-level divergence between two
runs of the same Poisson trace at different budgets is the canonical
example). The dumped `output_dump/` directories per scenario are
therefore *snapshots*, not regression baselines.

M4 introduces no new bench-level config knob that changes the forward
path; the harness is host-side glue. M2's deterministic-trace byte-
equality matrix (16/16 MATCH) and M3's deterministic-trace sweep
(24/24 MATCH) cover the bench's correctness surface and carry forward
to M4 unchanged. **No new byte-equality run is required at M4
acceptance.**

If at some later point we want a byte-equality regression net for
the harness itself (e.g. catch a `gen_workload.py` token-shape change
that breaks tokenization), the right place is a small deterministic
trace pinned at an `arrival_time=0`-only YAML, run on every CI green
build.

## What is left for M4-paper

These are not blockers for the M4 milestone-acceptance gate but matter
for a paper-quality pass:

- Replace the 3-LoRA pool with 50 distinct r=16 LoRAs (HF download +
  `convert_lora_to_gguf.py`, same as M1).
- Run scenarios at the spec's 600 s duration to populate enough
  Poisson tail for TTFT p99 / p99.9 to stabilise.
- Add a budget sweep within each scenario (the M3 trick) — current
  acceptance is at unbounded; cache-hit story is missing without
  squeezing.
- Pull energy traces (`dumpsys batterystats` per spec §4.M4 stretch
  goal). Currently skipped.
